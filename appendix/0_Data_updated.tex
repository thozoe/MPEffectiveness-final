\scriptsize \textbf{Notes:} The inflation-linked swaps are gathered from Macrobond, while the remaining EA variables are obtained from the ECB's Statistical Data Warehouse (\href{https://sdw.ecb.europa.eu/}{https://sdw.ecb.europa.eu/})\rev{, now the ECB Data Portal (\href{https://data.ecb.europa.eu/}{data.ecb.europa.eu})}. Relative to the previous version, this implies (i) the revised, COVID-adjusted natural rate estimates published by the Federal Reserve Bank of New York (through 2026:Q1); (ii) the October 2025 vintage of the policy surprise factors (see the footnote in \autoref{sec:measuring}); (iii) the aggregate CISS spliced with its updated composite after May 2025; (iv) the EONIA series continued with the euro short-term rate (EuroSTR) plus 8.5 basis points after its discontinuation in January 2022; and (v) an extended euro area inflation-linked swap panel that now spans the entire sample (earlier vintages ended in May 2022), with values on the overlapping period identical to the previous vintage. As a consequence, the full system---bond yields, inflation swaps, and the macro-financial block---is estimated jointly on the single sample running from $2005$:W$1$ to the end of $2025$; the sample split used in earlier versions for the inflation-swap and real-rate results is no longer necessary. The two alternative US stance metrics (\texttt{SSR} and \texttt{EMS}) are taken from the June 2026 vintage of the same source and likewise cover the full sample.}
